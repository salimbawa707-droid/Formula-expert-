Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Detergent Formula Expert ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your data when using our Progressive Web App or Google Play Store application targeting Android API Level 36.

1. Data Safety & Collected Information

We are committed to data minimization and transparency in accordance with the 2026 Google Play Developer Program policies:

  • Local Storage (First-Party): Your formulas, batch calculations, lab observations, and inventory stock are processed and stored locally on your device via secure IndexedDB storage (Dexie.js).
  • Cloud Services & Providers:
    • Google Firebase: Used for secure identity authentication (Google Sign-In) and optional encrypted cloud backup (Cloud Firestore). Data is scoped strictly to your authenticated user account.
    • Google Gemini API (Server-Proxied): Used for chemical troubleshooting and formulation assistance. Queries are proxied server-side without transmitting personal credentials or device telemetry.
  • Sensitive Permissions: This application does not access sensitive device metadata, contacts, location logs, SMS, or private phone resources. Camera access is strictly requested locally for optical character recognition (OCR) and barcode/label scanning of chemical raw materials, and no camera frames are stored or transmitted.

2. Play Store Compliance & Account Deletion

In full compliance with Google Play Store guidelines for account control:

  • User-Initiated Deletion: Any registered user can permanently delete their account and purge all synchronized back-ups immediately from within the application interface under Settings / Account Security.
  • Complete Data Purge: Deleting your account performs a complete purge of your email details, synced formulas, active logs, and local device databases.

3. Security Standards

We use modern cryptographic standards (HTTPS/TLS 1.3) to protect your synced data in transit. We never sell, lease, or distribute your proprietary formulations to third parties.
