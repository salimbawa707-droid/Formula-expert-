# Hu et al. (2023) — License and Data-Availability Recheck

**Audit date:** 2026-10-25  
**Source:** Hu et al., “Effective Detergency Determination for Single Polymeric Fibers Using Confocal Microscopy,” *Polymers* 15(15), 3314 (2023), DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/polym15153314  
**Disposition:** ARTICLE LICENSE CONFIRMED; RAW DATA NOT PUBLICLY AVAILABLE; DATA INGESTION BLOCKED.

## Verified publisher / repository statements

1. The publisher article page identifies the article as open access and states that the article is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) license. The full-text record on PubMed Central likewise identifies CC BY. Reuse of article content must preserve attribution and comply with the license, including any third-party material exceptions.
2. The article's Data Availability Statement says the data are available from the corresponding authors upon reasonable request. The accessible article record does not provide a downloadable row-level raw replicate dataset for the measurements described.
3. The paper reports a method using confocal microscopy and summarizes selected detergency outcomes. These abstract/conclusion summaries are not a replicate-level calibration table and cannot be transformed into pseudo-observations.
4. The study describes experimental conditions including fiber type, detergent system, temperature, water hardness and flow/wash timing. Those conditions would need to be preserved alongside each measured record if data are later obtained.

## Evidence boundary

- **Article/license metadata:** SOURCE_BACKED for the publisher's stated CC BY article license and data availability statement.
- **Raw experimental dataset:** NOT AVAILABLE IN PUBLIC ARTICLE RECORD / NOT INGESTED.
- **Numeric figure digitization:** NOT PERFORMED.
- **Model training/calibration or accuracy claims:** NOT PERMITTED from the current evidence.
- **LAB_VALIDATED:** NOT GRANTED.

The article's CC BY license does not create access to raw data that are not published. If the corresponding authors provide a dataset, separately record the received files, transfer date, data-specific reuse conditions, hashes, schema, units, test conditions, sample/replicate identifiers, missingness, and a reproducible audit before any ingestion. Do not infer that permission to reuse the article automatically settles every condition attached to separately supplied data.

## Phase decision

This recheck resolves the article-license metadata question but **does not resolve the raw-data access blocker**. Keep the source as a methods/reference candidate only. Phase 4B remains OPEN; Phase 5 and Phase 6 remain LOCKED.
