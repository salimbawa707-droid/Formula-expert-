## Potential scientific relevance
The paper reports standardized soil-removal testing on textile under several household washing scenarios. It describes four Empa cotton soil fabrics: carbon black/olive oil, red wine, blood/milk/ink, and chocolate. Reported method details include SIST EN 60456:2005, ECE reference detergent 98, 2.45 kg cotton ballast, 10 L wash bath, 30-minute wash, a 3 L/10-minute rinse, brightness measurements with a Datacolor Spectraflesh 600, and soil-removal assessment based on the CIE L* brightness difference between washed and unwashed standard soil. The paper says experiments used five parallel experiments and three replicates. Its abstract identifies 40°C at 10 g/L as the strongest tested scenario for standard soil removal.

These are source-reported method descriptions and summary findings, not independently re-extracted raw replicate observations. Do not treat the abstract’s ranking as a numeric calibration dataset.

## Rights assessment
The publisher PDF and institutional repository record identify the work as licensed CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (Attribution–NonCommercial–NoDerivatives). This does not authorize commercial reuse or transformation of its data for the commercial Detergent Formula Expert app. No separate written permission was found in this review.

**Gate outcome:** Do not ingest its tables, transcribe numeric results into the Evidence Vault, or use them for training/calibration unless explicit rights clearance is obtained or a separate legally suitable source is verified. Merely being open access is not sufficient.

## Integrity and limits
- Publisher page/PDF and repository record were checked for bibliographic identity and licence information.
- The article’s methods and abstract were reviewed for relevance.
- No supplementary file was downloaded or parsed during this review.
- No table values or numeric measurements were ingested.
- No hashes were recorded because no source file was downloaded for preservation.
- No independent human review was performed.
- No accuracy, empirical validation, or `LAB_VALIDATED` status is claimed.

## Next action
Continue searching for directly relevant fabric soil-removal datasets with accessible numeric source data, documented experimental conditions/replication, verifiable provenance, and an explicit licence compatible with commercial reuse and any required transformation.
