# Phase 4B External Source Screening — 2026-10-23

## Purpose and decision rule

This is a source-screening report for the Detergent Formula Expert Evidence Vault. It is not a declaration that Phase 4B is complete, and it does not unlock Phase 5.

A source is eligible for quantitative ingestion only after we record: (1) exact source/version and provenance, (2) reuse license and commercial-use permission, (3) raw-vs-derived status, (4) measurement unit and operational definition, (5) protocol and test conditions, (6) sample/replicate structure, (7) missingness and uncertainty, (8) checksum/version integrity, and (9) engine applicability. Product safety/hazard ratings are not detergency performance measurements.

## What this means for Phase 4B

### Use now (with strict limits)
1. **Zenodo 18961871** — use only for source-record lineage, unit-separated descriptive foam/rheology summaries and reproducible parser checks. It does not validate cleaning performance or formula rankings.
2. **Hu et al. 2023** — cite published protocol/results as source-backed evidence. Any numbers manually transcribed from figures must be labelled SOURCE_EXTRACTED and/or DERIVED_FROM_SOURCE, retain units/conditions, and carry an extraction audit. Do not fabricate raw replicates.
3. **Pušić et al. 2006** — prioritize retrieving and independently transcribing tables. Until then, retain BLOCKED_FOR_NUMERIC_INGESTION.
4. **RepOD 10.18150/MKCDUM** — re-open the canonical ODS and verify row/column counts, headers, formula cells, units, missingness and license; keep current readiness deficient until that audit is complete.

### Do not use as cleaning-performance labels
- Clean Label Project certification/purity ratings.
- EWG product hazard grades.
- Think Dirty ratings.
- Pharma MACO/swab/rinse-limit calculations.
- Commercial validation software marketing descriptions.
These measure or describe other things; mixing them into a Cleaning Score/CPI training target would cause label leakage and scientific category errors.

## Required work before Phase 5 can unlock

1. **Source integrity:** every dataset has exact canonical source/version, retrieval date, checksum, provenance, attribution, license evidence and commercial-reuse decision.
2. **Raw-vs-derived lineage:** preserve original cells/rows; identify formula-derived cells, image-derived measurements, aggregates and transcriptions separately. No double-counting.
3. **Unit and protocol separation:** no mixed-unit grand total; group by measurement family, test protocol, stain, substrate, detergent concentration, water hardness, temperature, wash time, equipment and replicate.
4. **Missingness:** unknown sample/row counts remain null; absent standard deviations are not zero; no imputed values enter measured validation partitions.
5. **Independent reproduction:** scripts regenerate committed artifacts from canonical source files and fail closed on discrepancies. Add regression tests for every historical claim that must remain unresolved.
6. **Applicability and validation:** a source can support only the engine and conditions it actually measures. Require held-out measured data, no train/test leakage by source/study/group, documented metrics and acceptance criteria before model promotion.
7. **Gate review:** resolve the 27,982 historical aggregate claim or formally document why it cannot be reproduced; independently review all Phase 4B acceptance criteria. Phase 5 stays locked until the authoritative roadmap gate is satisfied.

## Current conclusion

This screening found useful source leads but **does not close Phase 4B**. The most relevant sources are Zenodo 18961871 for foam/rheology and the Hu et al. 2023 article for limited detergency method/results; Pušić et al. 2006 remains a high-priority multi-stain dataset candidate whose numeric extraction is still blocked. The consumer rating apps and pharmaceutical cleaning-validation sites are not substitutes for detergent cleaning-performance measurements.

No engine is promoted to LAB_VALIDATED, no 95–98% accuracy claim is made, and Phase 5 remains locked.
