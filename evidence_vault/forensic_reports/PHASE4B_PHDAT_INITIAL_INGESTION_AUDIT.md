## Scientific applicability

PhDat represents aqueous surfactant phase behavior versus temperature and composition. It may support carefully scoped phase-behavior or compatibility research, with source-defined phase labels and applicability limits retained.

It is **not** direct ground truth for detergent cleaning performance, soil removal, hard-water chelation, foam lifetime, pH, Cleaning Score, formula rankings, or formula accuracy. The dataset includes literature-curated/digitized diagrams; parse success and hashes do not mean every value is a new raw laboratory measurement.

No engine eligibility or scientific maturity is promoted. Keep complete and incomplete records distinct; preserve source/figure/method/purity metadata; keep measured, source-extracted/digitized, derived, estimated, and predicted evidence classes separate.
