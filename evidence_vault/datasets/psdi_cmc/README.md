## Inventory and initial structural audit

- `NIST_Table_of_Critical_Micelle_Concentration_Values.csv`: 4,990 data rows; fields include compound number, source CMC text, normalized value/unit slots, temperature, method, author/reference codes, and evaluation metadata.
- `CMC_Compounds.csv`: 719 compound rows with name, IUPAC, SMILES, molecular weights, and a method/provenance field.
- In the 4,990-row measurement file, 4,559 rows have numeric `CMC Value 1`. The NIST NSRDS 36 unit, source, evaluation, and quality legends are mapped in [`nist-codebook.json`](./nist-codebook.json). Observed concentration units include `M` = molar, `D` = wt/vol %, `W` = molal, `P` = wt %, `N` = normal, `S` = mol/kg solution, and `R` = value varied during experiment. `R` is not a convertible unit. No unit conversions were applied; preserve the heterogeneous source values and codes.
- The source also has free-text `CMC` cells and reference codes. Preserve all fields; do not parse date-like/reference codes as concentrations or collapse multiple measurement methods/temperatures into one value.
- All 719 compound numbers have matching table keys, but this does not validate names or structures. PSDI's compound 2 is a confirmed text/structure conflict: NIST's printed table names it “SODIUM OCTYL 1 SULFATE,” while the PSDI LLM-added IUPAC/SMILES encode branched 2-ethylhexyl sulfate. The isomers have the same nominal molecular weight. The discrepancy is recorded in [`compound-identity-review.csv`](./compound-identity-review.csv); no structure correction is applied pending source-paper review. The compound table marks 530 identities as LLM-initialized, 58 as mass lookups, 13 as LLM retries, 115 as unknown, and 3 as PubChem names.
- The unit legend is in the [original NIST/NBS NSRDS 36 publication](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/NSRDS/nbsnsrds36.pdf) (concentration-units legend, p. 222).

## Structural audit

A structural inventory is archived in [`structural-audit.json`](./structural-audit.json). The NIST `Evaluation` codes `1`, `2`, `D`, `3`, `L`, `P`, `R`, and `X` are now decoded; 4,951 rows carry recognized codes, 24 are blank, and 15 contain irregular values. The two-letter material/method quality codes are mapped; 4,700 rows have recognized pairs, 273 are blank, and 17 are irregular. NIST `Source` symbols are mapped, including `M` for units converted from the preceding value; that value is derived and is not an independent laboratory measurement. For the reference field, 4,964 of 4,973 five-digit values match the original reference index. Six code-63021 rows are bibliographically resolved through PubMed (PMID 14134118; DOI 10.1111/j.2042-7158.1963.tb12887.x), although article measurement data were not ingested and reuse rights were not assessed. Three rows remain unresolved: code 45005 (two rows) and 69016 (one row), listed in [`reference-code-review.csv`](./reference-code-review.csv). Full definitions and observed counts are in [`nist-codebook.json`](./nist-codebook.json). This is structural and codebook reconciliation, not a row-by-row transcription check against the full volume.\n\n## Use gate

This is an openly licensed literature-derived data collection, not a new app laboratory campaign. The full 4,990-row measurement archive remains quarantined: do not bulk-load it into ingredient master data, calibrate a model, or claim engine validation. A single, separately curated row is recorded in [`curated_reference_slice.csv`](./curated_reference_slice.csv) and registered in [`../source-register.csv`](../source-register.csv): sodium dodecyl sulfate (SLS), 0.0082 M at 25 °C, NIST evaluation 1, material/method quality AA, surface-tension linear-plot method, reference 66007. Its citation resolves to Elworthy & Mysels (1966), DOI 10.1016/0095-8522(66)90017-1. The app uses it only as a pure-component SLS CMC input parameter. It does not validate mixed CMC predictions, surfactant synergy, finished formulas, or any product category. The compound table's AI-added structure fields are not used. The dataset's AI-assisted extraction history is documented on the [PSDI resource page](https://resources.psdi.ac.uk/data/a7c82670-d2e2-46c6-920a-74294289aa34), licensed CC BY 4.0.

Dimethyldodecylamine oxide (NIST compound 21): 0.0021 M at 27 °C, evaluation 3, quality BB, turbidity/light-scattering plot, reference 62005 resolved to Herrmann, J. Phys. Chem. 66, 295 (1962), DOI 10.1021/j100808a025. An independent listing row (reference 64016, 25 °C) gives 0.0020 M. It replaces the earlier unsourced 0.20 mM lauramine-oxide default, which the open-reference benchmark found about 10x low. Same scope limits as the SLS row. A filtered homolog slice used for the plausibility screen lives in [`../../open_reference_benchmarks/cmc_pure`](../../open_reference_benchmarks/cmc_pure); the full archive stays quarantined.
